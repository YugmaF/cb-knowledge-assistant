## When to Use This Runbook

Use the Kafka Consumer Lag Recovery runbook when a consumer group on the payment-events Kafka topic falls behind. Consumer lag on payment-events delays merchant settlement, balance postings, notifications and statements. Alerts fire when settlement consumer lag exceeds 100,000 messages or grows for more than 10 minutes.

## Step 1: Measure the Consumer Lag

1. Run `kafka-consumer-groups.sh --bootstrap-server kafka.platform:9092 --describe --group settlement-consumer` and record total lag and lag per partition.
2. Check whether lag is spread across all partitions (a consumer throughput problem) or on a few partitions (a hot key or stuck consumer).
3. Check broker health: disk usage on every broker, under-replicated partitions, and recent leader elections.

## Step 2: Find the Cause of the Lag

Common causes of payment-events consumer lag:

1. Consumer group rebalancing: consumers exceed max.poll.interval.ms because downstream calls such as the core-banking-gateway are slow. Look for "member left group" in consumer logs.
2. Broker problems: a full broker disk or repeated partition leader elections.
3. Downstream slowness: core banking in batch read-only mode, or the core-banking-gateway rate limiting consumers.

## Step 3: Recover

1. For rebalancing, reduce max.poll.records (for example from 500 to 100) and raise max.poll.interval.ms, then roll the consumers.
2. For a full broker disk, delete expired segments, expand the volume, and confirm retention is 7 days: `kafka-configs.sh --describe --entity-type topics --entity-name payment-events`.
3. Scale settlement consumers up to the partition count (48) to increase throughput: `kubectl -n platform scale deploy/settlement-consumer --replicas=48`.
4. Never reset consumer offsets to latest on payment-events; skipping messages means lost settlements. Offset changes need approval from the payments incident commander.

## Step 4: Verify

The payment-events lag is recovered when total lag is under 1,000 messages and stable for 15 minutes. Ask payments operations to confirm that settlement files for the day are complete and reconcile with payments-ledger totals.

## Related Incidents

Payment-events Kafka consumer lag incidents: INC-2025-018 and INC-2026-020. Escalate to the Kafka platform engineer if lag is still growing after 30 minutes.
