## When to Use This Runbook

Use the NationalSwitch Timeout Failover runbook when the nationalswitch-connector reports timeouts or errors from NationalSwitch, the interbank switch. Symptoms include interbank transfer failures, InstaPay payments to other banks stuck pending, payee name enquiry failures, and NationalSwitch p99 latency above 3 seconds.

## Step 1: Confirm the NationalSwitch Problem

1. Check the nationalswitch-connector dashboard for timeout rate per call type (transfer, instapay, name_enquiry).
2. Check the circuit breaker state: `curl -s http://nationalswitch-connector.payments:8080/actuator/circuitbreakers`. The circuit breaker should be CLOSED normally and OPEN during a NationalSwitch outage.
3. Call the NationalSwitch operations desk to confirm whether NationalSwitch has a known issue, maintenance or data centre failover.

## Step 2: Stop Any Retry Storm

A NationalSwitch retry storm happens when the connector keeps retrying failed calls and multiplies load on NationalSwitch. To stop a retry storm:

1. Confirm the circuit breaker is enabled for every call type, including name_enquiry; see INC-2026-022.
2. If any call type is retrying, set its retries to zero: `kubectl -n payments set env deploy/nationalswitch-connector NS_RETRY_MAX_NAME_ENQUIRY=0` (repeat per call type).
3. If NationalSwitch has started rate limiting Commercial Bank, ask the NationalSwitch operations desk to lift the limit once retries are stopped.

## Step 3: Fail Over to the NationalSwitch DR Site

If NationalSwitch announces failover to its disaster recovery site:

1. Switch the connector endpoint: `kubectl -n payments set env deploy/nationalswitch-connector NS_ENDPOINT=https://dr.nationalswitch.example`.
2. Restart the connector to drop stale connections to the primary site.
3. Confirm a successful name enquiry on a test account before closing the circuit.

## Step 4: Customer Communication

While NationalSwitch is unavailable, enable the mobile and internet banking banner "Transfers to other banks are delayed" via the flag `banner_interbank_delay`. Transfers inside Commercial Bank are not affected by a NationalSwitch outage.

## Step 5: Recovery

When NationalSwitch recovers, the circuit breaker moves to HALF_OPEN and then CLOSED automatically. Restore retry settings to the defaults (max 2 retries, exponential backoff with jitter), switch back to the primary endpoint if a DR failover was used, and confirm pending transfers have drained.
