## Purpose

The On-Call Escalation runbook explains who to page and when during an incident at Commercial Bank. The On-Call Escalation runbook supports the response SLAs in the Incident Management Policy (POL-003). Each tier 1 service has a primary on-call engineer listed in the service catalogue.

## Step 1: Acknowledge the Page

1. The primary on-call engineer acknowledges the page within 5 minutes for SEV1 and 15 minutes for SEV2.
2. If the primary on-call engineer does not acknowledge in time, the paging system automatically pages the secondary on-call engineer.
3. If neither acknowledges within 15 minutes, the paging system pages the engineering manager for the service.

## Step 2: Assess Severity and Declare

1. Assess severity using the SEV1 to SEV4 definitions in POL-003.
2. Declare the incident in the incident channel with the command `/incident declare sev2 "<short title>"`.
3. For SEV1 or SEV2, page the Incident Manager on duty to act as incident commander.

## Step 3: Escalate by Area

Escalate to the right team using these on-call rotations:

1. Payments services (payments-api, payments-ledger, cardnet-gateway-adapter, nationalswitch-connector): payments on-call, then Head of Payments Engineering.
2. Kafka, DNS, core-banking-gateway, SSO platform infrastructure: platform on-call, then Head of Platform Engineering.
3. Ledger database and PgBouncer: database reliability on-call.
4. Security events, phishing, HSMs and suspected breaches: security operations on-call, then the CISO.
5. Mobile app and mobile-bff: retail digital on-call.
6. Data warehouse and dwh-etl: data engineering on-call.

## Step 4: Executive and Regulatory Escalation

For every SEV1, the incident commander notifies the CTO and the Chief Risk Officer within 30 minutes. For a SEV1 or a payments SEV2, operational risk assesses regulatory notification within 2 hours. For a confirmed data breach, the CISO informs the Data Protection Officer immediately.

## Step 5: External Parties

Contact external parties through the vendor escalation list: CardNet Gateway support for card authorisation problems, the NationalSwitch operations desk for interbank switch problems, and PayCore support for payment processing software defects. Never share customer data with vendors over chat or email.

## Step 6: Handover

On-call engineers hand over at 09:00 every Monday. Open incidents, overdue postmortem action items and planned changes during freeze windows must be included in the on-call handover note.
