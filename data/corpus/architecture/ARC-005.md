## Overview

The Identity and SSO Architecture describes how Commercial Bank authenticates staff, customers and services. The SSO platform is owned by the identity engineering team in security, led by Omar Haddad. The SSO platform runs two separate identity tenants: a staff tenant for employees and contractors, and a customer tenant for mobile and internet banking customers. Keeping the tenants separate means a staff SSO outage does not stop customer logins.

## Staff SSO

Staff SSO provides single sign-on to internal applications such as the HR portal, the incident tracker, the enterprise AI assistant and the branch teller application. Staff SSO uses OpenID Connect and SAML, with number-matching multi-factor authentication for all staff. Staff SSO roles are mapped from HR job titles and department, and joiner, mover and leaver changes flow automatically from the HR system.

## Customer Identity

The customer identity tenant authenticates mobile and internet banking customers. Customer identity issues short-lived signed access tokens (15 minutes) and refresh tokens bound to the device. Signing keys are rotated every 90 days, and new public keys are published in the JWKS endpoint 48 hours before they become active so that relying services such as mobile-bff can cache them.

## Roles for the Enterprise AI Assistant

The enterprise AI assistant receives the user's role from staff SSO as a token claim. Three roles are defined: viewer (public and internal documents), analyst (adds confidential documents) and admin (adds restricted documents). Restricted access additionally requires membership of the specific approval group for each restricted document.

## Service Accounts and Secrets

Services authenticate to each other with short-lived tokens from the SSO platform using the client credentials flow. Privileged service accounts, such as batch accounts used by the core banking integration, have credentials stored in the secrets vault with automatic rotation every 30 days. Secrets must never appear in CI logs; the CI system masks registered secrets.

## High Availability

The SSO platform runs active-active in two data centres for the token service, with a primary and replica database per tenant. Database replica lag is monitored and alerts above 60 seconds. The recovery time objective for staff SSO is 30 minutes and for customer identity 15 minutes.

## Monitoring and Threat Detection

Security operations monitors SSO sign-in logs for impossible travel, logins from unrecognised hosts for service accounts, password spraying and credential stuffing. Sign-in logs are retained for 12 months under the Information Security Policy.
