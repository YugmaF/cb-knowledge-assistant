## Overview

The Mobile Banking Architecture describes the Commercial Bank mobile banking app for iOS and Android and the backend services behind it. The mobile banking app serves about 1.2 million active customers and supports balance enquiries, transfers, InstaPay, bill payments, card controls and real-time fraud alerts. The mobile banking platform is owned by the retail digital team led by Ishara Rathnayake.

## Mobile App Clients

The mobile banking app clients are native iOS and Android applications. The mobile app stores no account data at rest except an encrypted device binding key in the platform keystore. Releases ship every two weeks, with staged rollout through the app stores starting at 10 percent of users. Feature flags allow server-side control of new mobile features.

## mobile-bff

The mobile-bff (backend for frontend) is the only backend the mobile banking app talks to. The mobile-bff aggregates calls to payments-api, the core-banking-gateway, the fraud-engine and the notification service, and shapes responses for mobile screens. The mobile-bff is a tier 1 service running 10 pods behind a web application firewall (WAF) with rate limiting on login and payment endpoints.

## Authentication and Sessions

Mobile banking login uses the customer identity tenant of the SSO platform. The mobile app sends credentials or a biometric-unlocked device key to the mobile-bff, which obtains signed access tokens from SSO. The mobile-bff validates token signatures using the SSO public keys (JWKS) and must refresh the JWKS cache whenever a token carries an unknown key id. Transfers from a newly bound device require a one-time password (OTP). Login protection includes bot detection, per-IP rate limits and progressive delays rather than hard account lockout.

## Payments from the Mobile App

Mobile banking payments go from the mobile app to the mobile-bff and then to payments-api. The mobile-bff never talks to payments-ledger directly. Each payment request carries an idempotency key generated on the device, so a retried mobile payment is never duplicated.

## Notifications

The mobile banking notification service consumes the payment-events Kafka topic and fraud-engine alerts, and sends push notifications through the mobile operating system push services. Real-time fraud alerts use a high-priority notification channel with a target delivery time of under 10 seconds.

## Observability

The mobile banking platform tracks login success rate, payment success rate, and app crash rate per release. The mobile-bff alerts when login success drops below 90 percent for five minutes. Mobile incidents are handled by the retail digital on-call engineer.

## Security Controls

The mobile banking app uses certificate pinning, root and jailbreak detection, and code obfuscation. The mobile-bff enforces device binding and step-up authentication for new payees and high-value transfers.
