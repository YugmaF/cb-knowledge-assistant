## Purpose and Attendees

Project Falcon is the confidential codename for Commercial Bank's evaluation of acquiring Harbourline Finance, a fictional digital consumer lender with about 310,000 customers. This Project Falcon evaluation was presented to the Board Risk Committee on 2026-08-18. Attendees: Anjali Bandara (Chief Risk Officer), Nirmala Wijesinghe (CTO), Sofia Marquez (CISO), Thomas Whitfield (Operational Risk), and the board members of the committee. Project Falcon information is restricted and market sensitive; distribution is limited to the named Project Falcon approval group.

## Strategic Rationale

The Project Falcon rationale is to accelerate growth in digital personal lending, where Commercial Bank has a small market share. Harbourline Finance has a mobile-only lending app, an automated credit scoring engine and a customer base that is 70 percent under 35 years old. Project Falcon would give Commercial Bank a ready-made digital lending platform and about 18 months of time-to-market advantage over building one.

## Valuation

The Project Falcon financial evaluation values Harbourline Finance at between 4.2 and 4.8 billion in the bank's base currency, based on a discounted cash flow model and comparable transactions. The recommended maximum offer for Project Falcon is 4.5 billion, with 20 percent of the price deferred and linked to loan book performance over two years.

## Risk Assessment

The Project Falcon risk assessment found three main risks. Credit risk: the Harbourline Finance loan book has a non-performing loan ratio of 6.8 percent, above the bank's appetite of 4 percent. Technology risk: Harbourline Finance runs on a single public cloud region with no disaster recovery site, and its customer identity platform would need migration to the bank's SSO. Regulatory risk: the acquisition needs regulator approval, expected to take 6 to 9 months.

## Integration Plan

The Project Falcon integration plan keeps the Harbourline Finance brand for 12 months, migrates customers to the Commercial Bank mobile app in phase 2, and moves loan servicing onto the bank's core banking system within 24 months. Estimated one-off integration cost for Project Falcon is 380 million.

## Recommendation and Decision

The Board Risk Committee recommended that Project Falcon proceed to confirmatory due diligence, with a non-binding offer of up to 4.5 billion, subject to an independent review of the Harbourline Finance loan book and a cyber security assessment. Final approval of Project Falcon is reserved for the full board in November 2026. No public announcement is permitted before board approval.
