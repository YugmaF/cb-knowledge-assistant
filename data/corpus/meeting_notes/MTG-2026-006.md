## Attendees and Goals

The Q3 2026 planning meeting for payments and platform was held on 2026-06-29. Attendees: Daniel Okafor, Hannah Lindqvist, Nadeesha Silva, Grace Mwangi, Kevin Adeyemi, Yusuf Karim, Elena Petrova. The goal of Q3 2026 planning was to balance new payments features with the reliability work from the incident reviews.

## Capacity Allocation

For Q3 2026, payments engineering capacity is split 55 percent product features, 30 percent reliability and postmortem action items, and 15 percent technical debt and upgrades. Platform engineering keeps 40 percent for reliability. The 30 percent reliability allocation for payments continues from Q1 2026 because five action items are still overdue.

## Payments Product Priorities

The Q3 2026 payments product priorities are: (1) InstaPay bulk payee validation for small businesses, launching before the August salary day; (2) Card Tokenization for merchant card-on-file, phase 2; (3) Mobile Bill Payments scheduled payments; (4) Real-time Fraud Alerts SMS fallback. Nadeesha Silva confirmed InstaPay bulk payee validation will ship behind a feature flag with canary rollout.

## Reliability Priorities

The Q3 2026 reliability priorities are: extend the NationalSwitch circuit breaker to all call types; add Kafka broker disk alerts and capacity review for retention changes; audit all deployment pipelines for canary coverage, including fraud rules; migrate zone PIN keys to key block format so the HSM firmware upgrade can proceed; and a salary-day readiness review before 2026-08-25.

## Risks

The Q3 2026 planning meeting recorded risks. The August salary day falls one week after the planned bulk payee validation launch, leaving little time to observe its load on payments-ledger. The HSM key migration depends on security team capacity. The enterprise AI assistant pilot needs the security review before the payments team can use it for postmortem search.

## Enterprise AI Assistant Pilot

The Q3 2026 plan includes a pilot of the enterprise AI assistant with payments and platform on-call engineers. Success measures for the pilot: engineers find the right runbook in under one minute, and analysts can ask trend questions across postmortems such as "what are the recurring root causes of payment failures in the last year". Yusuf Karim will provide the document corpus and an evaluation set.
