## Attendees and Scope

The June 2026 monthly incident review was held on 2026-06-24, chaired by Elena Petrova, Incident Manager. Attendees: Nirmala Wijesinghe, Daniel Okafor, Hannah Lindqvist, Mei Chen, Ashan Dissanayake, Tharindu Jayasuriya, Grace Mwangi, Thomas Whitfield. The June incident review looked at payment incident trends over the twelve months to June 2026.

## Payment Incident Trends

The June incident review counted 17 payment failure incidents since May 2025. By root cause category: payments-ledger database connection pool exhaustion 4 (INC-2025-003, INC-2025-015, INC-2025-023, INC-2026-004); TLS certificate expiry on the payment gateway 3 (INC-2025-006, INC-2025-020, INC-2026-012); NationalSwitch timeouts and retry storms 3 (INC-2025-009, INC-2025-025, INC-2026-009); bad configuration or feature flag deployment without canary 3 (INC-2025-011, INC-2025-029, INC-2026-014); payment-events Kafka consumer lag 2 (INC-2025-018, INC-2026-020); DNS misconfiguration 1 (INC-2026-002); HSM firmware 1 (INC-2026-007). Five root causes account for 15 of the 17 payment incidents.

## What Improved

The incident review noted progress on recurring payment root causes. Automated certificate rotation went live on 2026-04-30 after the third certificate expiry, and no certificate incident has happened since. PgBouncer now runs in transaction pooling mode and the payments-ledger read replica is live, although salary day in August is the first real test. The nationalswitch-connector circuit breaker is on by default for transfers.

## What Still Worries Us

The incident review flagged remaining risks. Configuration changes keep bypassing the canary through side pipelines, such as the fraud rule pipeline in INC-2026-014. The NationalSwitch circuit breaker still does not cover name enquiry. Kafka capacity changes, like the retention increase behind INC-2026-020, are made without capacity review. Daniel Okafor warned that new InstaPay features could create new load patterns on payments-ledger before the August salary day.

## Action Item Health

Of 41 postmortem action items raised for payment incidents since May 2025, 29 are done, 7 are in progress and 5 are overdue. The median time to close a payments action item is 71 days against a target of 30 days. The CTO asked for overdue items to be reported at every CAB meeting.

## Decisions and Action Items

- Extend the circuit breaker to name enquiry before the July peak (owner: Malith Wickramasinghe).
- Include every new InstaPay feature in the August salary-day load test (owner: Tharindu Jayasuriya).
- Require capacity review for Kafka retention and partition changes (owner: Mei Chen).
- Audit all deployment pipelines for canary coverage (owner: Hannah Lindqvist, due 2026-07-31).
- Next monthly incident review: 2026-07-22.
