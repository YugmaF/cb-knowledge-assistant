## Attendees and Format

The payments team retrospective on the August salary day was held on 2026-09-01, facilitated by Elena Petrova. Attendees: Daniel Okafor, Kasun Perera, Priya Raman, Tharindu Jayasuriya, Malith Wickramasinghe, Nadeesha Silva, Ashan Dissanayake. The payments team retrospective used the format "what went well, what did not go well, what we will change", and focused on INC-2026-028, the fifth payments-ledger connection pool exhaustion incident.

## What Went Well

The payments team retrospective recorded what went well on the August salary day. Detection was fast: the PgBouncer client wait queue alert fired within 2 minutes. The salary credit batch finished at 06:40, before the retail peak, as planned. Card payments were not affected, and the new NationalSwitch circuit breaker on all call types kept interbank transfers failing fast instead of hanging. The feature flag on bulk payee validation meant the fix was a flag change rather than a deployment during the freeze.

## What Did Not Go Well

The payments team retrospective recorded what did not go well. The salary-day load test ran on 2026-08-10, before bulk payee validation was enabled, so the N+1 query pattern was never tested at salary-day volume. It took 50 minutes to connect the PgBouncer saturation to the new feature because ledger query metrics were not broken down by feature. Several team members felt that each salary day brings a new variation of the same payments-ledger capacity problem, and that fixes treat symptoms rather than the ledger design.

## Team Health

The payments team retrospective discussed on-call load. Payments on-call engineers handled 11 incidents in the last quarter, above the team target of 6, and two engineers were paged on consecutive salary days. The team asked for a secondary on-call engineer on every salary day and month-end, and for salary day on-call duty to be counted as overtime.

## What We Will Change

The payments team agreed these changes: every feature that touches payments-ledger declares a connection budget and a query count per request in its design review; the salary-day load test runs within 3 days of salary day with every enabled flag; ledger query metrics are tagged by feature; and a payments-ledger capacity redesign is commissioned with platform engineering, due 2026-10-31.

## Action Items

- Tag payments-ledger query metrics by feature (owner: Priya Raman, due 2026-09-30).
- Move the salary-day load test within 3 days of salary day (owner: Tharindu Jayasuriya).
- Add a secondary payments on-call engineer for salary day and month-end (owner: Daniel Okafor).
- Start the payments-ledger capacity redesign (owner: Hannah Lindqvist and Ashan Dissanayake, due 2026-10-31).
