## Attendees and Agenda

The Architecture Review Board (ARB) met on 2026-03-11, chaired by Nirmala Wijesinghe (CTO). Attendees: Daniel Okafor, Hannah Lindqvist, Sofia Marquez, Omar Haddad, Ashan Dissanayake, Malith Wickramasinghe, Yusuf Karim. The ARB agenda had three items: circuit breaker thresholds for the nationalswitch-connector, the payments-ledger read replica design, and the enterprise AI assistant for internal documents.

## Item 1: NationalSwitch Circuit Breaker Thresholds

The ARB reviewed the nationalswitch-connector circuit breaker, which shipped disabled in February and had to be enabled manually during INC-2026-009. The ARB approved these circuit breaker settings: open when more than 50 percent of calls in a 30-second sliding window fail or time out, stay open for 20 seconds, then allow 10 trial calls in half-open state. Retries use exponential backoff with jitter and a maximum of 2 retries. The ARB decided the circuit breaker is enabled by default. Malith Wickramasinghe noted the circuit breaker currently covers only the transfer call type; name enquiry will follow later in the quarter.

## Item 2: Payments-Ledger Read Replica

The ARB approved a PostgreSQL read replica for payments-ledger balance queries to take load off the primary on salary day. Balance reads may be up to 2 seconds stale; reservations and postings always use the primary. Ashan Dissanayake will deliver the read replica by 2026-03-31. The ARB also asked for a connection budget per feature on payments-ledger, because PgBouncer alone will not protect the ledger from new query patterns.

## Item 3: Enterprise AI Assistant

The ARB reviewed a proposal for an enterprise AI assistant that answers staff questions from internal documents such as policies, runbooks and postmortems. The ARB required: retrieval must enforce the Data Classification Policy by role (viewer, analyst, admin) before any text reaches the model; restricted documents must never be retrieved for viewers; every answer must cite source doc_ids; and the assistant must treat document content as data, not instructions, to defend against prompt injection. Sofia Marquez asked for a test set that includes a poisoned document.

## Decisions

The ARB decisions: approve the nationalswitch-connector circuit breaker settings and default-on; approve the payments-ledger read replica; approve a pilot of the enterprise AI assistant for payments and platform teams, subject to a security review.
