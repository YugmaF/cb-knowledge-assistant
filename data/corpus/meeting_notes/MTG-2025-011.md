## Attendees and Scope

The December 2025 monthly incident review was held on 2025-12-15 and chaired by Elena Petrova, Incident Manager. Attendees: Nirmala Wijesinghe (CTO), Daniel Okafor (Head of Payments Engineering), Hannah Lindqvist (Head of Platform Engineering), Ashan Dissanayake, Tharindu Jayasuriya, Mei Chen, Thomas Whitfield (Operational Risk). The December incident review covered all payment incidents since May 2025.

## Payment Incident Summary Since May 2025

The December incident review counted nine payment failure incidents between May and early December 2025: three payments-ledger connection pool exhaustion incidents (INC-2025-003, INC-2025-015, INC-2025-023), two payment gateway TLS certificate expiries (INC-2025-006, INC-2025-020), two NationalSwitch timeout retry storms (INC-2025-009, INC-2025-025), one bad fee configuration deployment (INC-2025-011) and one payment-events Kafka consumer lag incident (INC-2025-018). Two of the nine were SEV1: the June CardNet Gateway certificate expiry and the November salary day outage.

## Recurring Root Causes

The incident review agreed that most payment incidents repeat a small number of root causes. Payments-ledger connection pool exhaustion happens on every salary day or month-end peak because PgBouncer and a read replica were never delivered. TLS certificate expiry happened twice because automated certificate rotation from INC-2025-006 was deprioritised for InstaPay. NationalSwitch retry storms happened twice because the circuit breaker from INC-2025-009 was not built. The CTO said the pattern shows postmortem action items are written but not funded.

## Overdue Action Items

The December incident review listed overdue action items: automated certificate rotation for the cardnet-gateway-adapter (due 2025-09-30, not started), PgBouncer for the ledger database (due 2025-11-15, in progress), nationalswitch-connector circuit breaker (due 2025-10-31, not started), and payment-events partition increase (done). Under the Incident Management Policy, INC-2025-020, INC-2025-023 and INC-2025-025 are flagged as repeat incidents and escalated to the CTO.

## Decisions

The incident review decided: (1) 30 percent of payments engineering capacity in Q1 2026 is reserved for reliability action items; (2) no new InstaPay features ship until PgBouncer is live; (3) the Change Management Policy will be updated in January 2026 to require canary rollout for all payments changes, including configuration and feature flags; (4) salary day and month-end become change freeze windows.
