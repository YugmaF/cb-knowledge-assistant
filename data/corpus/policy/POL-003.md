## Purpose and Scope

The Incident Management Policy defines how Commercial Bank detects, classifies, responds to and learns from incidents that affect customers, staff or bank systems. The Incident Management Policy applies to all production services, including payments, mobile banking, core banking, data platforms and internal tools. The policy owner is the Head of Platform Engineering.

## Severity Definitions SEV1 to SEV4

Every incident is assigned a severity from SEV1 to SEV4 under the Incident Management Policy. SEV1 is a critical incident: a full outage of a tier 1 service such as payments or card authorisation, a confirmed data breach, or impact on more than 10,000 customers. SEV2 is a major incident: partial degradation of a tier 1 service or significant customer impact with a workaround. SEV3 is a minor incident: degradation of a tier 2 or tier 3 service, or internal staff impact only. SEV4 is a low-impact incident with no customer impact, such as a delayed internal report. When in doubt, declare the higher severity and downgrade later.

## Response SLAs

The Incident Management Policy sets these response SLAs. SEV1: on-call acknowledges within 5 minutes, an incident commander is assigned within 15 minutes, executives are notified within 30 minutes, and status updates are posted every 30 minutes. SEV2: acknowledge within 15 minutes, incident commander within 30 minutes, status updates every hour. SEV3: acknowledge within 1 hour during business hours. SEV4: handle by the next business day. For any SEV1 or payments SEV2, the operational risk team must assess regulatory notification within 2 hours.

## Roles During an Incident

During a SEV1 or SEV2 incident, the incident commander coordinates the response and makes decisions, a communications lead updates the status page and contact centre, and subject matter experts investigate. The incident commander may be the Incident Manager on duty or a senior engineer. Escalation follows the On-Call Escalation runbook (RB-006).

## Postmortems

A blameless postmortem is mandatory for every SEV1 and SEV2 incident and must be published within 5 business days of resolution. The postmortem uses the standard sections: Summary, Impact, Timeline, Root Cause, Resolution and Action Items. Every action item must have a named owner and a due date. SEV3 incidents need a postmortem when the root cause has occurred before.

## Action Item Tracking and Recurrence

Postmortem action items are tracked in the incident tracker. Overdue action items are reported at the monthly incident review meeting. If an incident has the same root cause as an earlier incident whose action items were not completed, the incident is flagged as a repeat incident and escalated to the CTO.

## Compliance

Breaches of the Incident Management Policy, such as missing postmortems or unreported SEV1 incidents, are reported to the Operational Risk Committee.
