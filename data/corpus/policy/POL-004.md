## Purpose and Scope

The Change Management Policy controls how changes are made to Commercial Bank production systems so that change-related incidents are reduced. The Change Management Policy covers code deployments, configuration changes, feature flag changes, infrastructure changes, DNS changes and firmware upgrades, including HSM firmware. Version 2.0 of the Change Management Policy was issued on 2026-01-15 after several payment incidents caused by configuration and feature flag changes deployed without a canary.

## Change Types

The Change Management Policy defines three change types. A standard change is pre-approved, low risk and repeatable, such as scaling pods. A normal change needs risk assessment and approval by the Change Advisory Board (CAB). An emergency change fixes an active incident or imminent risk and is approved by the incident commander and the Head of Engineering for the area, then reviewed at the next CAB.

## Change Advisory Board (CAB)

The Change Advisory Board meets every Tuesday and Thursday. Normal changes must be submitted to CAB at least two business days before the planned date with a risk assessment, test evidence and a rollback plan. Changes to payments services, HSMs, DNS for payment services, and core banking always need CAB approval and cannot be standard changes.

## Canary Requirement for Payments Changes

Every change to a payments service must use a canary rollout. Payments services include payments-api, payments-ledger, cardnet-gateway-adapter, nationalswitch-connector, fraud-engine and payment-events consumers. The canary must receive no more than 5 percent of traffic for at least 30 minutes with automated health checks and automatic rollback before full rollout. Feature flags for payments must roll out in steps of 1, 10, 50 and 100 percent. Emergency changes to payments still require a canary of at least 5 minutes. Configuration changes and fraud rule changes count as payments changes under the canary requirement.

## Freeze Windows

The Change Management Policy defines change freeze windows when only emergency changes are allowed. Salary day freeze: from two business days before the 25th of each month (or the next business day if the 25th is a weekend) until one business day after. Month-end freeze: the last two business days of each month. Year-end freeze: 20 December to 5 January. Exceptions during a freeze window require approval from both the Head of Payments Engineering and the CTO.

## Rollback

Every change must have a tested rollback plan. For payments services, rollback follows the Rolling Back a Bad Deployment runbook (RB-005) and must be possible within 15 minutes.

## Compliance

Changes made outside the Change Management Policy are reported to the CAB and the Operational Risk Committee. A change that causes a SEV1 or SEV2 incident without following the Change Management Policy triggers a mandatory review of the approving team's change rights.
