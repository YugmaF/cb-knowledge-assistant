## Purpose and Scope

The Information Security Policy sets the minimum security requirements for all Commercial Bank staff, contractors and systems. The Information Security Policy applies to every application, device, network and cloud service that stores or processes bank or customer information, whether operated by the bank or by a third party on its behalf. The policy owner is the Chief Information Security Officer (CISO), Sofia Marquez, and the Information Security Policy is reviewed at least once a year.

## Roles and Responsibilities

Under the Information Security Policy, the CISO owns the security programme and reports security risk to the Board Risk Committee every quarter. System owners are accountable for the security of their systems, including patching, access reviews and logging. Every employee and contractor is responsible for protecting credentials, reporting suspected security incidents to security@commbank.example within one hour, and completing annual security awareness training.

## Access Control

Access to Commercial Bank systems follows least privilege and need-to-know. All staff access goes through the corporate SSO with multi-factor authentication (MFA); number-matching MFA is mandatory for all staff from 2026-01-31. Privileged access to production requires just-in-time elevation that expires after 8 hours. Access rights are reviewed quarterly by system owners, and leavers' access must be removed within 24 hours of their last working day. Shared accounts are prohibited except for service accounts registered with the security team.

## Passwords and Secrets

Staff passwords must be at least 14 characters and must not be reused across systems. Service account credentials, API keys and certificates must be stored in the bank secrets vault and must never be written to source code, CI logs, tickets or chat. Privileged service account credentials must be rotated automatically at least every 90 days. Any exposure of a secret is a security incident and must be reported immediately.

## Encryption

Commercial Bank data classified confidential or restricted must be encrypted at rest using AES-256 or stronger and in transit using TLS 1.2 or higher. Cryptographic keys for payment card and PIN processing are held only in the hardware security modules (HSMs). TLS certificates used by customer-facing and payment services must be tracked in the certificate inventory with automated expiry monitoring.

## Logging and Monitoring

Security-relevant events, including logins, privilege elevation and access to restricted data, must be logged centrally and retained for at least 12 months. Security operations monitors alerts 24x7. Logs must not contain passwords, full card numbers or other secrets.

## Compliance and Exceptions

Breaches of the Information Security Policy may lead to disciplinary action. Exceptions to the Information Security Policy must be requested in writing, risk assessed by the security team, approved by the CISO, and reviewed at least every six months.
