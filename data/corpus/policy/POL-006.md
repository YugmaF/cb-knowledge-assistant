## Purpose and Scope

The Leave and Remote Work Policy explains leave entitlements and remote working arrangements for permanent Commercial Bank employees. Contractors follow the terms in their contracts. The Leave and Remote Work Policy is owned by the Head of People, Chamari Senanayake.

## Annual Leave Entitlement

Under the Leave and Remote Work Policy, full-time employees receive 21 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, rising to 24 days after five years of service. Part-time employees receive annual leave in proportion to their contracted hours. Annual leave is requested and approved in the HR portal.

## Annual Leave Carry-Over

Employees may carry over up to 5 unused days of annual leave into the next calendar year. Carried-over annual leave must be used by 31 March, after which it expires. Leave requests for carry-over must be submitted in the HR portal before the year-end deadline announced by HR each December.

## Sick Leave

Employees receive 14 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required for sick leave longer than two consecutive working days. Sick leave cannot be carried over to the next year.

## Parental and Other Leave

Commercial Bank provides 16 weeks of paid maternity leave and 4 weeks of paid paternity leave. Employees may also take up to 3 days of paid compassionate leave for the death of a close family member, and 2 days of paid study leave per exam for approved professional qualifications.

## Remote Work Eligibility

The Leave and Remote Work Policy allows hybrid working for roles that do not require a physical presence. Eligible employees may work remotely up to 2 days per week, agreed with their manager. Branch, contact centre and data centre roles are generally not eligible for remote work. New joiners may start remote work after completing their first 3 months.

## Remote Work Rules

Employees working remotely must use a bank-issued laptop, connect through the bank VPN, and work from a private space where screens cannot be seen by others. Printing bank documents at home is not allowed. Remote work from outside the country requires prior approval from HR and the security team and is limited to 10 working days per year.

## On-Call and Salary Day Attendance

Engineers on the on-call rota may be asked to work on site during salary day and month-end freeze windows. Managers may cancel remote work days for business needs with at least 2 working days of notice.

## Requests and Approvals

All leave and remote work requests are submitted in the HR portal and approved by the employee's line manager. Disputes about the Leave and Remote Work Policy are escalated to the People team at people@commbank.example.
