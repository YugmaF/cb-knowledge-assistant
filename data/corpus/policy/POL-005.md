## Purpose and Scope

The Acceptable Use of AI Tools Policy sets rules for how Commercial Bank staff and contractors may use artificial intelligence tools, including chat assistants, code assistants and AI features built into other software. The Acceptable Use of AI Tools Policy applies to both approved internal AI tools, such as the enterprise AI assistant, and external AI tools accessed over the internet.

## Approved AI Tools

Staff may use AI tools only if they appear on the approved AI tools list maintained by the security team. The enterprise AI assistant is the approved tool for questions about internal documents. Any new external AI tool must pass a security and privacy assessment before use. Browser extensions that send page content to AI services are prohibited on bank devices.

## No Customer PII in External AI Tools

Staff must never enter customer personally identifiable information (PII) into an external AI tool. Customer PII includes names combined with account numbers, national identity numbers, card numbers, phone numbers, addresses, transaction histories and any data that identifies a customer. Staff must also not enter confidential or restricted information, source code for payments systems, credentials or API keys into external AI tools. Only public and internal information may be used with approved external AI tools.

## Using the Enterprise AI Assistant

The enterprise AI assistant only returns documents the user is entitled to see based on the Data Classification Policy (POL-002). Staff must not try to bypass access controls, for example by asking the enterprise AI assistant to ignore its instructions or to reveal restricted documents. Answers from the enterprise AI assistant must be checked against the cited source documents before being used in decisions, customer communications or regulatory submissions.

## Human Accountability

AI output is advice, not a decision. The member of staff who uses an AI tool remains accountable for the result. AI tools must not be used to make final credit, fraud, employment or disciplinary decisions without human review.

## Reporting Misuse and Suspicious Content

Staff must report to security@commbank.example any accidental entry of customer PII into an AI tool, and any document or email that contains hidden instructions aimed at AI assistants, such as text telling an AI tool to ignore its instructions or to include a link in its answers. Such prompt injection content is treated as a security event.

## Compliance

Breaches of the Acceptable Use of AI Tools Policy may result in removal of AI tool access and disciplinary action. Entering customer PII into an external AI tool is treated as a data protection incident under the Incident Management Policy.
