## Summary

SEV3 security incident. On 2025-11-14 a phishing campaign impersonating a "PayCore invoice portal" reached 140 finance and treasury staff. Six staff clicked the phishing link and two entered their SSO credentials on a fake login page.

## Impact

The phishing campaign compromised the passwords of two staff accounts. MFA blocked all login attempts made with the stolen credentials from overseas IP addresses. No customer data was accessed and no payments were initiated.

## Timeline

- 10:12 Staff member reports a suspicious invoice email to the security mailbox.
- 10:40 Security operations lead Dilini Gunawardena confirms 140 recipients from look-alike domain paycore-invoices.example.
- 11:05 Emails purged from all mailboxes; domain blocked at the email gateway and web proxy.
- 12:30 Two staff who entered credentials identified from proxy logs; passwords reset and sessions revoked.
- 15:30 Sign-in logs reviewed; no successful attacker login; incident resolved.

## Root Cause

The phishing emails were delivered because the email gateway did not quarantine mail from newly registered look-alike domains. The fake page copied the bank SSO login screen. MFA push approval, not number matching, was in use, which leaves staff exposed to MFA fatigue in future phishing attacks.

## Resolution

The phishing emails were purged, the domain was blocked, and the two affected accounts had passwords reset and sessions revoked.

## Action Items

- Quarantine email from domains registered in the last 30 days (owner: Dilini Gunawardena).
- Move SSO MFA to number matching (owner: Omar Haddad, due 2026-01-31).
- Run targeted phishing awareness training for finance staff.
