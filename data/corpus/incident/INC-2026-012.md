## Summary

SEV2 incident. On 2026-04-02 the intermediate CA certificate in the chain presented by the cardnet-gateway-adapter expired at 06:00. The CardNet Gateway rejected TLS handshakes and e-commerce card payments failed from 06:10 to 07:25. This was the third payment gateway certificate expiry, after INC-2025-006 and INC-2025-020.

## Impact

The CardNet Gateway intermediate certificate expiry declined e-commerce card payments for about 11,800 customers. Point-of-sale transactions used a separate chain and were not affected.

## Timeline

- 06:10 Alert: e-commerce authorisation success rate below 40 percent.
- 06:25 SEV2 declared; handshake errors show an expired intermediate certificate.
- 07:05 Updated certificate chain deployed using RB-001.
- 07:25 Authorisations normal; incident resolved.

## Root Cause

The CardNet Gateway certificate expiry recurred because expiry monitoring checked only leaf certificates, not intermediate certificates, and automated certificate rotation was still not in production eight months after first being promised in INC-2025-006.

## Resolution

The certificate chain was updated manually and the cardnet-gateway-adapter pods were restarted.
