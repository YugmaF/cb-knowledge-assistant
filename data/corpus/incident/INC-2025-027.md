## Summary

SEV3 incident. On 2025-12-18 the HR portal was unavailable from 09:00 to 13:40, the week of the year-end leave carry-over deadline. Staff could not submit or approve leave requests in the HR portal.

## Impact

The HR portal outage had no customer impact. About 1,900 staff could not access the HR portal, and HR extended the leave carry-over deadline from 2025-12-19 to 2025-12-22.

## Timeline

- 09:00 Staff report HR portal errors to the service desk.
- 09:45 Platform on-call finds the HR portal database volume 100 percent full.
- 11:30 Audit log table archived and compressed.
- 13:40 Disk extended; HR portal back online; incident resolved.

## Root Cause

The HR portal outage was caused by an uncompressed audit log table filling the database disk. The HR portal is a tier 3 service and had no disk usage alert.

## Resolution

The HR portal database disk was extended and old audit logs were archived to object storage.

## Action Items

- Add disk usage alerts to all tier 3 databases, including the HR portal (owner: Ruwan Fernando).
- Apply a 90-day retention policy to the HR portal audit log table (owner: Lucas Moreau).
