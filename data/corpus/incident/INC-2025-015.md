## Summary

SEV2 incident. On 2025-09-30, the September month-end, the payments-ledger service ran out of database connections again, four months after the May salary-day incident INC-2025-003. Payments failed intermittently from 16:20 to 18:05.

## Impact

The month-end payments-ledger connection pool exhaustion affected about 7,800 customers and 40 corporate clients whose bulk payment files were delayed. Retail transfers failed intermittently for 1 hour 45 minutes.

## Timeline

- 16:20 Alert: payments-api 5xx rate above 5 percent.
- 16:35 Ledger database refuses new connections; max_connections of 1000 reached.
- 16:50 SEV2 declared; database reliability engineer Ashan Dissanayake joins.
- 17:30 Corporate bulk payment job paused; payments-ledger scaled from 10 to 7 pods.
- 18:05 Error rate normal; bulk job resumed at reduced concurrency; incident resolved.

## Root Cause

The payments-ledger connection pool exhaustion recurred because corporate bulk payment jobs and retail traffic shared one pool, and bulk jobs held long transactions. After INC-2025-003 the pool was raised to 120 connections per pod, so 10 pods could request 1,200 connections against a database limit of 1,000; raising the pool made exhaustion worse. The PgBouncer evaluation from INC-2025-003 had not started.

## Resolution

The payments-ledger incident was resolved by pausing the bulk payment job, reducing pods so total connections fitted under the database limit, and resuming bulk processing at low concurrency.

## Action Items

- Give bulk payment jobs a separate, smaller connection pool (owner: Priya Raman).
- Deploy PgBouncer in front of the ledger database (owner: Ashan Dissanayake, due 2025-11-15).
- Schedule corporate bulk files outside retail peak hours at month-end.
