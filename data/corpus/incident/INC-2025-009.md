## Summary

SEV2 incident. On 2025-07-08 NationalSwitch, the interbank switch, slowed down and the nationalswitch-connector responded with a retry storm. Interbank transfers failed or stayed pending from 14:10 to 15:32.

## Impact

The NationalSwitch timeout incident affected about 6,300 customers whose interbank transfers failed or remained pending for over an hour. Idempotency keys prevented duplicate transfers. Transfers between accounts inside Commercial Bank were not affected by the NationalSwitch problem.

## Timeline

- 14:10 Alert: nationalswitch-connector p99 latency above 5 seconds.
- 14:25 NationalSwitch operations confirm degraded response times during maintenance on their side.
- 14:40 SEV2 declared; connector thread pool at 100 percent.
- 15:05 Retry count reduced from 5 to 1 by configuration change and pods restarted.
- 15:20 NationalSwitch latency returns to normal.
- 15:32 Pending transfers drained; incident resolved.

## Root Cause

NationalSwitch response times rose from 300 ms to 6 seconds. The nationalswitch-connector retried every failed call 5 times immediately, with no backoff and no circuit breaker. The retries multiplied traffic to NationalSwitch about six-fold, NationalSwitch started rate limiting the bank, and the connector thread pool of 200 threads saturated.

## Resolution

The NationalSwitch retry storm was stopped by reducing retries to 1 and restarting the nationalswitch-connector. Pending interbank transfers completed once NationalSwitch recovered.

## Action Items

- Implement a circuit breaker in the nationalswitch-connector (owner: payments engineering, due 2025-10-31).
- Use exponential backoff with jitter for NationalSwitch retries.
- Subscribe the payments on-call rota to NationalSwitch maintenance notices.
