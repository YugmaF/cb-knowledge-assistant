## Summary

SEV2 incident. On 2026-03-04 a NationalSwitch latency spike caused the third nationalswitch-connector retry storm, and interbank transfers failed from 11:45 to 13:05.

## Impact

The NationalSwitch timeout incident affected about 7,400 customers with failed interbank transfers. The new bulkhead thread pools kept name enquiries working, so customers could still add payees.

## Timeline

- 11:45 Alert: NationalSwitch timeouts above 15 percent.
- 12:00 SEV2 declared; retries tripling NationalSwitch traffic.
- 12:20 Circuit breaker found built but disabled by configuration.
- 12:40 Circuit breaker enabled manually; connector stops calling NationalSwitch while open.
- 13:05 NationalSwitch recovered; circuit closed; incident resolved.

## Root Cause

The circuit breaker from INC-2025-025 had been built but shipped disabled, waiting for an Architecture Review Board decision on thresholds. Without the circuit breaker, NationalSwitch timeouts again produced a retry storm.

## Resolution

The NationalSwitch retry storm ended once the circuit breaker was enabled manually and NationalSwitch latency recovered.

## Action Items

- Agree circuit breaker thresholds at the March Architecture Review Board (owner: Daniel Okafor).
- Enable the nationalswitch-connector circuit breaker by default (owner: Malith Wickramasinghe).
