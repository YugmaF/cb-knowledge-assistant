## Summary

SEV2 security incident. On 2026-03-19 a credential stuffing attack from more than 3,000 IP addresses hit the mobile app login API. Account lockouts triggered by the attack caused mobile app login failures for legitimate customers from 19:40 to 23:10.

## Impact

The credential stuffing attack locked out about 15,000 customers. The attacker logged in successfully to 38 customer accounts using passwords reused from a third-party breach; no payments were made from those accounts because new-device logins require an OTP for transfers.

## Timeline

- 19:40 Alert: mobile login failures 20 times normal.
- 20:05 Security operations confirms credential stuffing from residential proxy networks.
- 20:45 WAF rate limits and IP reputation blocking applied to the mobile-bff login endpoint.
- 22:00 Attack traffic below 1 percent of peak.
- 23:10 14,200 locked accounts unlocked after checks; incident resolved.

## Root Cause

The mobile app login failures were caused by per-username lockout after 5 failed attempts, which an attacker can trigger for any customer. The mobile-bff login endpoint had no bot detection and no per-IP rate limiting.

## Resolution

Attack traffic was blocked at the WAF, locked accounts were bulk unlocked, and the 38 compromised accounts were forced to reset passwords.

## Action Items

- Add bot detection and device fingerprinting to the mobile-bff login flow (owner: Kevin Adeyemi).
- Replace hard lockout with progressive delays and step-up verification (owner: Omar Haddad).
- Check customer passwords against known breached password lists.
