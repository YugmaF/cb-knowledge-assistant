## Summary

SEV3 incident. On 2026-02-10 the nightly data warehouse ETL (dwh-etl) ran eight hours late, delaying the daily regulatory liquidity report from 05:30 to 14:10.

## Impact

The data warehouse ETL delay had no customer impact. The regulatory liquidity return was submitted under a four-hour extension agreed with the regulator, and treasury worked from the previous day's position until 14:10.

## Timeline

- 05:30 Alert: dwh-etl liquidity aggregate not complete.
- 06:10 Data on-call Sachini Herath finds warehouse compute at its autoscaling maximum.
- 08:00 New fraud analytics workload found running in the same batch window.
- 09:15 Fraud analytics jobs paused; ETL restarted with full capacity.
- 14:10 Liquidity report delivered; incident resolved.

## Root Cause

The data warehouse ETL delay was caused by resource contention. A new fraud analytics workload was scheduled into the nightly batch window without capacity review, and the warehouse compute cluster hit its autoscaling limit, starving the dwh-etl regulatory jobs.

## Resolution

The fraud analytics jobs were paused and the dwh-etl regulatory jobs rerun with priority. Fraud analytics moved to a separate compute pool the following week.

## Action Items

- Run regulatory dwh-etl jobs in a dedicated, reserved compute pool (owner: Yusuf Karim).
- Require capacity review for any new job in the nightly batch window.
