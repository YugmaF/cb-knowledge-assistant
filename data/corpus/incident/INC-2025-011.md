## Summary

SEV3 incident. On 2025-08-19 a configuration change to the interbank transfer fee table was deployed to every payments-api pod at once, without a canary stage. The bad fee configuration charged customers ten times the correct fee between 10:05 and 10:58.

## Impact

The bad fee configuration affected 950 customers, who were charged a fee of 250.00 instead of 25.00 on interbank transfers. All 950 customers were refunded by 2025-08-21. There was no impact on transfer success rates.

## Timeline

- 10:05 Fee configuration change deployed to all payments-api pods.
- 10:31 Contact centre reports customers complaining about high transfer fees.
- 10:40 Payments engineering identifies the fee table change as the cause.
- 10:52 Previous configuration version redeployed.
- 10:58 Fees verified correct on test transfers; incident resolved.

## Root Cause

The bad configuration deployment was caused by a decimal place error in the payments-api fee YAML file. Configuration files were not peer reviewed, had no schema validation, and were deployed straight to production without a canary. Configuration changes were also outside the scope of change advisory board review at that time.

## Resolution

The fee configuration was reverted to the previous version and the 950 overcharged customers were refunded through a bulk adjustment on 2025-08-21.

## Action Items

- Require a canary stage for all payments-api configuration changes (owner: Daniel Okafor).
- Add schema and range validation for fee configuration (owner: Priya Raman).
- Bring payments configuration changes under the change management process.
