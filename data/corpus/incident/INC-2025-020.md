## Summary

SEV1 incident. On 2025-11-03 the server TLS certificate on the payment gateway ingress (pay-gw.commbank.example), which the CardNet Gateway uses to deliver authorisation callbacks, expired at 09:00. Card authorisations failed from 09:14 to 10:31. This was the second payment gateway certificate expiry in five months after INC-2025-006.

## Impact

The payment gateway certificate expiry declined e-commerce and point-of-sale card transactions for about 21,400 customers over 77 minutes, during the Monday morning peak.

## Timeline

- 09:14 Alert: card authorisation success rate below 20 percent.
- 09:25 CardNet Gateway support reports handshake failures to the bank callback endpoint.
- 09:40 SEV1 declared; expired ingress certificate identified.
- 10:15 Renewed certificate deployed to the payment gateway ingress.
- 10:31 Authorisations back to normal; incident resolved.

## Root Cause

The payment gateway TLS certificate expired because the automated certificate rotation promised after INC-2025-006 was never delivered; the work was deprioritised in favour of InstaPay. The expiry monitoring added after INC-2025-006 covered only the cardnet-gateway-adapter client certificate, not the ingress certificate.

## Resolution

The payment gateway ingress certificate was renewed manually using RB-001 and card authorisations recovered.
