## Summary

SEV3 incident. On 2026-05-29 the core banking month-end interest accrual batch overran by three hours. The core-banking-gateway stayed in read-only mode longer than planned and e-statement generation started late.

## Impact

The core banking batch overrun delayed e-statements for about 48,000 customers by one day. Payments were unaffected because the core-banking-gateway queued postings during read-only mode.

## Timeline

- 04:30 Alert: core banking batch still running past its 04:00 target.
- 05:15 Platform on-call confirms interest accrual running slowly for a new savings product.
- 07:10 Batch completes; core-banking-gateway leaves read-only mode.
- 09:45 Queued postings applied; statement generation rescheduled; incident resolved.

## Root Cause

The core banking batch overrun was caused by a new savings product with daily tiered interest, which tripled the accrual calculation time. The product launch did not include a batch window impact assessment.

## Resolution

The core banking batch was allowed to finish, queued postings were applied, and e-statements were generated the next night.

## Action Items

- Add batch window impact assessment to the new product checklist (owner: Hannah Lindqvist).
- Parallelise the interest accrual step by product.
