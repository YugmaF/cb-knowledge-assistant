## Summary

SEV4 incident. On 2025-09-10 the nightly data warehouse ETL batch (dwh-etl) finished five hours late, so morning management dashboards showed the previous day's data until 11:20.

## Impact

The data warehouse ETL delay had no customer impact. Branch performance dashboards were stale and the daily liquidity report was sent at 11:30 instead of 08:00.

## Timeline

- 06:15 dwh-etl completion check fails; data on-call Sachini Herath alerted.
- 07:00 Loans extract job found stuck after a schema validation failure at 02:10.
- 09:30 Mapping updated for a new column in the core banking loans extract.
- 11:20 ETL rerun completes; dashboards refreshed; incident resolved.

## Root Cause

The data warehouse ETL delay was caused by an unannounced schema change in the core banking loans extract, which added a new column. The dwh-etl loans job failed schema validation and waited for a manual restart, and alerting only checked for batch completion at 06:15 rather than for job failure.

## Resolution

The data engineer updated the dwh-etl mapping for the new column and reran the loans job and downstream aggregates.

## Action Items

- Agree a schema contract with the core banking team for extract files (owner: Yusuf Karim).
- Alert on dwh-etl job failure immediately, not only on late completion.
