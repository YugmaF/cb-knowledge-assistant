## Summary

SEV1 security incident. On 2026-05-12 security monitoring detected the privileged service account svc-core-batch authenticating from an unrecognised host. The svc-core-batch password had been exposed in a CI build log readable by contractor accounts. The credential exposure was contained on 2026-05-13 at 18:00.

## Impact

The credential exposure gave read access to core banking batch schedules and account master data for about 40 minutes. Forensic review found no evidence of customer data exfiltration, but the regulator was notified as a precaution under the 72-hour rule.

## Timeline

- 2026-05-12 03:20 Alert: svc-core-batch login from host outside the batch subnet.
- 2026-05-12 03:45 SEV1 declared; CISO Sofia Marquez notified.
- 2026-05-12 04:00 svc-core-batch disabled; batch jobs paused.

- 2026-05-13 18:00 Credentials rotated, forensics complete; incident resolved.

## Root Cause

The svc-core-batch credential exposure was caused by a build script that printed environment variables in debug mode, writing the service account password into a CI log. Contractor accounts had read access to all CI logs, and the service account was not vaulted or rotated automatically.

## Resolution

The svc-core-batch account was disabled, its credential rotated into the secrets vault, the CI log purged, and the contractor account involved suspended pending investigation.

## Action Items

- Move all privileged service account credentials into the secrets vault with automatic rotation (owner: Omar Haddad).
- Mask secrets in CI logs and restrict contractor access to CI logs (owner: Ruwan Fernando).
- Complete the HR and legal investigation of the contractor account (owner: Sofia Marquez).
