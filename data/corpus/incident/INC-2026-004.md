## Summary

SEV2 incident. On 2026-01-26, the January salary day (moved from Sunday 25 January), the payments-ledger service again exhausted its database connections from 09:05 to 10:40, even though PgBouncer had been deployed a week earlier.

## Impact

The January salary day payments-ledger incident affected about 12,600 customers with failed or slow transfers and InstaPay payments. Salary credits completed on time because the batch had moved to 05:00.

## Timeline

- 09:05 Alert: payments-ledger pool wait time above 2 seconds.
- 09:20 SEV2 declared; PgBouncer shows every server connection busy.
- 09:50 Database reliability engineer finds PgBouncer running in session pooling mode.
- 10:25 PgBouncer switched to transaction pooling mode after a quick test in staging.
- 10:40 Error rate normal; incident resolved.

## Root Cause

The payments-ledger connection pool exhaustion recurred because PgBouncer was deployed in session pooling mode, which pins each client connection to a server connection and gives no multiplexing benefit. The PgBouncer rollout was never load tested at salary-day volume, and the read replica for balance queries was not yet in place.

## Resolution

The payments-ledger incident was resolved by switching PgBouncer to transaction pooling mode, which reduced server connections from 1,000 to about 180.
