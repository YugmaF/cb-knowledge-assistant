## Summary

SEV2 incident. On 2025-12-09 NationalSwitch timeouts during the evening peak triggered a second retry storm in the nationalswitch-connector. Interbank transfers and account name enquiries failed from 18:30 to 20:05.

## Impact

The NationalSwitch retry storm affected about 9,100 customers. Interbank transfers failed, and because name enquiries shared the same thread pool, customers could not add new payees from other banks.

## Timeline

- 18:30 Alert: nationalswitch-connector timeouts above 20 percent.
- 18:45 SEV2 declared; NationalSwitch reports a capacity problem at its data centre.
- 19:10 Connector retries reduced to zero; queued requests drained.
- 19:50 NationalSwitch capacity restored.
- 20:05 Transfers and name enquiries normal; incident resolved.

## Root Cause

The NationalSwitch retry storm recurred because the circuit breaker promised after INC-2025-009 was not implemented. Backoff had been added but retries remained at 3, and transfers and name enquiries shared one thread pool, so NationalSwitch timeouts on one call type blocked the other.

## Resolution

The retry storm was stopped by disabling nationalswitch-connector retries until NationalSwitch recovered.

## Action Items

- Implement the nationalswitch-connector circuit breaker (owner: Malith Wickramasinghe, due 2026-02-28). Carried over from INC-2025-009.
- Separate thread pools (bulkheads) for transfers and name enquiries.
- Write the NationalSwitch timeout failover runbook (became RB-003).
