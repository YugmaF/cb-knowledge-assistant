## Summary

SEV3 incident. On 2025-10-22 the settlement consumer group on the payment-events Kafka topic fell behind by 1.8 million messages. Merchant settlements and statement postings were delayed by up to six hours between 11:00 and 17:40.

## Impact

The payment-events Kafka consumer lag delayed settlement credits for about 3,100 merchant and customer accounts. Payments were accepted normally; only settlement and posting were late.

## Timeline

- 11:00 Alert: settlement consumer lag above 100,000 messages.
- 11:40 Platform on-call Ruwan Fernando sees the consumer group rebalancing every few minutes.
- 13:10 Kafka engineer Mei Chen links rebalances to slow core banking calls.
- 14:30 max.poll.records reduced from 500 to 100 and max.poll.interval.ms raised.
- 17:40 Lag back to zero; incident resolved.

## Root Cause

The payment-events Kafka consumer lag was caused by settlement consumers exceeding max.poll.interval.ms (5 minutes) while waiting on slow core banking calls. Kafka removed the consumers from the group, causing continuous rebalancing. The payment-events topic had only 12 partitions, which also limited parallelism.

## Resolution

The payment-events lag cleared after tuning max.poll.records and max.poll.interval.ms so consumers stayed in the group.

## Action Items

- Increase payment-events partitions from 12 to 48 (owner: Mei Chen).
- Alert on consumer lag growth rate, not only absolute lag.
- Write a Kafka consumer lag recovery runbook (became RB-004).
