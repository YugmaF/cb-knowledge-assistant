## Summary

SEV2 incident. On 2026-04-24 a fraud rule configuration change set the card velocity threshold to 1 transaction per hour instead of 10, and was deployed to every fraud-engine node without a canary. Legitimate card payments were declined from 12:00 to 13:30.

## Impact

The fraud rule configuration change declined second and later card transactions within an hour for about 9,800 customers during the lunchtime peak, and generated about 14,000 false fraud alerts.

## Timeline

- 11:58 Fraud rule change deployed to all fraud-engine nodes.
- 12:00 Alert: card decline rate doubles.
- 12:40 SEV2 declared; decline reason "velocity rule V-07" dominant.
- 13:15 Previous rule configuration restored.
- 13:30 Decline rate normal; incident resolved.

## Root Cause

The bad configuration deployment happened because fraud rules are deployed by a separate pipeline owned by the fraud team, which was not covered by the canary requirement the Change Management Policy introduced for payments changes in January 2026.

## Resolution

The fraud rule configuration was rolled back and customers who received false fraud alerts were sent an apology notification.

## Action Items

- Extend the payments canary requirement to fraud-engine rule configuration (owner: Grace Mwangi).
- Simulate rule changes against the previous 24 hours of transactions before deployment.
