## Summary

SEV2 incident. On 2026-01-12 an internal DNS misconfiguration pointed payments-ledger.internal at a decommissioned load balancer. payments-api could not reach the payments-ledger from 13:20 to 14:35 and payments failed.

## Impact

The DNS misconfiguration caused failed transfers, bill payments and InstaPay payments for about 8,700 customers over 75 minutes. Card payments were unaffected because the cardnet-gateway-adapter uses a different ledger endpoint.

## Timeline

- 13:02 DNS zone migration job runs for the internal zone.
- 13:20 Alert: payments-api connection errors to payments-ledger.
- 13:40 SEV2 declared; payments-ledger.internal resolves to an old address.
- 13:55 Correct DNS record restored.
- 14:35 Cached records expire (TTL 3600 seconds); payments normal; incident resolved.

## Root Cause

The DNS misconfiguration was caused by a zone migration script that copied a stale record for payments-ledger.internal from an old backup. The change had no validation against live service endpoints, and the one-hour TTL slowed recovery after the fix.

## Resolution

The payments-ledger DNS record was corrected and caches expired naturally; some payments-api pods were restarted to clear resolver caches early.

## Action Items

- Validate DNS changes against a list of critical payment service records before apply (owner: Ruwan Fernando).
- Reduce TTL on critical internal payment records to 300 seconds.
- Add synthetic checks that resolve and call payments-ledger every minute.
