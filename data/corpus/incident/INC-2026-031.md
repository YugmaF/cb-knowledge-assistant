## Summary

SEV3 incident, still open. Since 2026-09-20 at 10:15 the branch teller application has been freezing intermittently at about 30 of 85 branches, usually after four or more hours of use.

## Impact

The branch teller freezes have no failed customer transactions, but branch queues are longer because tellers must restart the teller client. Digital channels are not affected.

## Timeline

- 10:15 First branch reports teller client freezes to the service desk.
- 12:00 Freezes confirmed at 30 branches, all on teller client version 3.8.
- 14:30 Workaround issued: restart the teller client at lunch.
- 2026-09-22 Memory profiling started with branch systems engineer Pradeep Kumar.

## Root Cause

The branch teller freeze root cause is under investigation. The leading hypothesis is a memory leak in teller client 3.8 in the receipt preview component.

## Resolution

The branch teller incident is not yet resolved. Workaround: tellers restart the client at lunch; rollback to teller client 3.7 is being tested at two pilot branches.

## Action Items

- Complete memory profiling of teller client 3.8 (owner: Pradeep Kumar).
- Decide on rollback to 3.7 by 2026-09-30 (owner: Kevin Adeyemi).
