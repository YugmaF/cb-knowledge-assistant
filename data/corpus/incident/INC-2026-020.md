## Summary

SEV2 incident. On 2026-06-16 consumer lag on the payment-events Kafka topic reached 2.4 million messages after a broker disk filled. InstaPay settlement postings were delayed by up to six hours from 08:30 to 14:55. This was the second payment-events Kafka consumer lag incident after INC-2025-018.

## Impact

The payment-events consumer lag delayed settlement and balance updates for about 4,600 InstaPay recipients, who saw "pending" credits. Some customers contacted the call centre believing their payment had failed.

## Timeline

- 07:55 Kafka broker kafka-3 disk reaches 100 percent.
- 08:30 Alert: settlement consumer lag above 500,000 messages.
- 09:10 SEV2 declared; repeated partition leader elections on payment-events.
- 10:30 Old segments deleted; broker disk expanded.
- 14:55 Lag cleared following RB-004; incident resolved.

## Root Cause

The payment-events consumer lag was caused by a full broker disk. Retention on payment-events had been raised from 3 to 14 days after an audit request without capacity planning, and there was no broker disk usage alert. Partition leader elections repeatedly stalled consumers.

## Resolution

Broker disk was expanded, retention set to 7 days with archiving to object storage, and consumers were scaled out to clear the payment-events lag.

## Action Items

- Alert on Kafka broker disk usage above 75 percent (owner: Mei Chen).
- Require capacity review for Kafka retention changes.
- Archive payment-events to object storage for audit instead of long retention.
