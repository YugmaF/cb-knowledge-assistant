## Summary

SEV3 incident. On 2026-07-21 the staff SSO identity provider was unavailable from 08:10 to 09:25, so staff could not sign in to internal applications such as the HR portal and the incident tracker.

## Impact

The staff SSO outage had no direct customer impact; customer logins use a separate identity tenant. Branch staff already signed in kept working, but about 2,300 staff could not start new sessions.

## Timeline

- 08:10 Alert: staff SSO login error rate 100 percent.
- 08:20 Primary SSO database found unhealthy after a storage fault.
- 08:25 Automatic failover starts but replica is 40 minutes behind.
- 09:10 Replica caught up and promoted.
- 09:25 Staff SSO logins normal; incident resolved.

## Root Cause

The staff SSO outage lasted 75 minutes because the SSO database replica had fallen 40 minutes behind after a long-running maintenance job, and failover waited for it to catch up. Replica lag was not monitored.

## Resolution

The staff SSO database replica was promoted once caught up and the failed primary rebuilt.

## Action Items

- Alert on SSO database replica lag above 60 seconds (owner: Omar Haddad).
- Move SSO maintenance jobs to throttled mode.
