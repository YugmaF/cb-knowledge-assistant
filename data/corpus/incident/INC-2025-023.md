## Summary

SEV1 incident. On 2025-11-25, the November salary day, payments-ledger connection pool exhaustion caused a payments outage lasting 4 hours 45 minutes, from 08:05 to 12:50. This was the third payments-ledger connection pool incident of 2025.

## Impact

The salary day payments outage affected about 35,000 customers. Transfers, InstaPay payments and bill payments failed; card payments were slow. Salary credits were delayed by up to three hours. The incident was reported to the regulator as a major operational incident.

## Timeline

- 08:00 Salary credit batch starts, overlapping with the retail morning peak.
- 08:05 Alert: payments-api error rate above 10 percent.
- 08:30 SEV1 declared; ledger database CPU at 100 percent and pool wait times above 30 seconds.
- 09:40 InstaPay throttled at payments-api to 50 percent.
- 11:30 Ledger database moved to a larger instance class.
- 12:50 Error rate normal; incident resolved.

## Root Cause

The payments-ledger connection pool exhaustion recurred because PgBouncer, planned after INC-2025-015, slipped to Q1 2026. The salary credit batch was scheduled at 08:00, overlapping the retail peak, and new InstaPay traffic added about 30 percent to payments-ledger load. Balance queries still ran on the primary database.

## Resolution

The salary day outage was resolved by throttling InstaPay, scaling up the ledger database and restarting payments-ledger with a smaller per-pod pool.

## Action Items

- Deploy PgBouncer before the January salary day (owner: Ashan Dissanayake, due 2026-01-20).
- Add a read replica for payments-ledger balance queries (owner: platform team).
- Start salary credit batches at 05:00 (owner: Daniel Okafor).
- Add salary day to the change freeze calendar.
