## Summary

SEV2 incident. On 2026-07-07 NationalSwitch failed over to its disaster recovery site and timed out for 25 minutes. The nationalswitch-connector circuit breaker protected transfers, but the name enquiry path had no circuit breaker and caused a fourth NationalSwitch retry storm from 10:10 to 11:50.

## Impact

The NationalSwitch timeout incident affected about 8,200 customers. Interbank transfers failed fast with a clear message while the circuit was open, but payee validation hung, and the name enquiry retry storm delayed NationalSwitch recovery for the bank.

## Timeline

- 10:10 Alert: NationalSwitch timeouts above 50 percent; transfer circuit opens.
- 10:20 SEV2 declared; name enquiry retries at 8 times normal volume.
- 10:35 NationalSwitch completes failover but rate limits Commercial Bank.
- 11:20 Name enquiry retries disabled using RB-003.
- 11:50 Rate limit lifted; incident resolved.

## Root Cause

The NationalSwitch retry storm recurred because the circuit breaker was enabled only for the transfer call type. The name enquiry call type still retried 3 times with short timeouts. Stale connections to the old NationalSwitch site also added timeouts.

## Resolution

Name enquiry retries were disabled and the connector connection pool was refreshed to the NationalSwitch DR site.
