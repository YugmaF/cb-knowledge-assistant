## Summary

SEV2 incident. On 2025-10-07 customers could not log in to the mobile banking app between 07:30 and 09:15. The mobile app login failures followed a scheduled rotation of the SSO token signing key.

## Impact

The mobile app login failures affected about 22,000 customers, who saw a "session expired, please log in again" loop. Customers already logged in were logged out when their tokens refreshed. Internet banking was not affected.

## Timeline

- 07:30 Alert: mobile-bff login success rate below 60 percent.
- 07:50 Mobile engineering lead Kevin Adeyemi finds "invalid token signature" errors in mobile-bff.
- 08:20 Identity team confirms the SSO signing key was rotated at 07:25.
- 08:55 mobile-bff JWKS cache flushed and pods restarted.
- 09:15 Login success rate back to 98 percent; incident resolved.

## Root Cause

The mobile app login failures were caused by mobile-bff caching the SSO public signing keys (JWKS) for 24 hours without refreshing on an unknown key id. When the SSO signing key rotated, mobile-bff rejected every newly issued token.

## Resolution

The mobile-bff JWKS cache was flushed and the pods restarted, after which mobile app logins recovered.

## Action Items

- Refresh the JWKS cache in mobile-bff when a token carries an unknown key id (owner: Kevin Adeyemi).
- Publish new SSO signing keys 48 hours before they become active (owner: Omar Haddad).
